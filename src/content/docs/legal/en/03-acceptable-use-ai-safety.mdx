# Acceptable Use Policy & AI Safety Charter (AUP)

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Governing Authority: Perplexta AI Safety Board & **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).

---

## 1. Operational Philosophy & Compute Responsibility

**ViralLinkUp Limited** ("Perplexta") is dedicated to providing high-performance analytical and generative compute environments to technical professionals and enterprises, while upholding stringent safety safeguards. Access to platform APIs, cluster orchestration, and GPU nodes is a privilege conditioned upon continuous, rigorous compliance with this Charter.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Workload Categories

The utilization of Perplexta gateways, inference endpoints, or GPU clusters for any of the following activities is strictly and categorically prohibited without exception:

### 2.1 Child Sexual Exploitation & Abuse (CSAM/CSAE)
- Ingesting, processing, generating, facilitating, or attempting to query content depicting or promoting child sexual exploitation or abuse.
- **Mandatory Enforcement:** Immediate permanent account termination, forfeiture of remaining balance, and automated criminal evidence submission to the UK National Crime Agency (NCA) and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

### 2.2 Weapons, Terrorism & Dangerous Materials
- Generating synthesis instructions, formulas, or operational blueprints for Chemical, Biological, Radiological, or Nuclear (CBRN) weapons.
- Promoting, financing, or recruiting for designated violent organizations, hate groups, or campaigns inciting genocide.

### 2.3 Malware Development & Cyberwarfare
- Crafting ransomware payloads, surveillance tooling, stealth rootkits, or automated vulnerability exploitation scripts.
- Executing distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, unauthorized network vulnerability scanning, or automated credential stuffing.

### 2.4 Non-Consensual Deepfakes & Coordinated Disinformation
- Fabricating non-consensual sexually explicit depictions or deceptive deepfakes of real individuals.
- Launching automated political disinformation networks, fraudulent financial entity impersonations, or election interference campaigns.

### 2.5 Unsupervised High-Risk Decisions
- Automated medical diagnosis or treatment prescribing without authorized human clinical validation.
- Unlicensed legal advice designed to evade criminal statutory enforcement or money laundering controls.

---

## 3. Anti-Jailbreaking & Adversarial Defenses

Perplexta enforces a zero-tolerance policy against deliberate adversarial attacks on platform model safety:
1. **Prompt Injection:** Crafting hidden or multi-modal vectors aimed at overriding system safety directives or reading runtime container memory.
2. **Jailbreak Exploits:** Simulating fictional frameworks, hypothetical personas, or token manipulation designed to bypass harm mitigations.
3. **Context Poisoning:** Submitting malformed tokens or malformed arrays intended to crash the routing orchestrator or exhaust GPU tensor buffers.

---

## 4. Upstream Cloud & AI Alignment

Users acknowledge that workload processing aligns with the upstream terms of foundational providers, notably the Google Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, Cloudflare Security Standards, and the UK Online Safety Act 2023.
