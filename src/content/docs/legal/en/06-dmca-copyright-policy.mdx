## 2. Formal Takedown Notice Requirements

To be legally effective, an infringement notification must be delivered in writing to our Designated Copyright Agent and contain the following mandatory information:
1. **Signature:** Physical or verified cryptographic electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
2. **Identification of Work:** Precise identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (e.g., canonical URLs or direct documentation).
3. **Identification of Infringing Content:** Direct platform URLs pointing specifically to the material hosted on Perplexta or ViralBook requested to be removed or disabled.
4. **Contact Details:** Legal name, physical address, direct telephone number, and official email address of the notifying party.
5. **Good Faith Statement:** An express statement that you maintain a good faith belief that disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. **Perjury Penalty Statement:** A formal declaration, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

## 3. Counter-Notification & Content Restoration

If you maintain in good faith that your material was disabled or removed as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written Counter-Notification containing:
- Your verified contact information and physical or electronic signature.
- Identification of the material removed and the location where it previously appeared.
- A statement under penalty of perjury that you maintain a good faith belief that removal occurred through error or misidentification.
- Consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

Upon receipt of a legally compliant Counter-Notification, we promptly transmit a copy to the original complainant. The contested content will be restored in ten to fourteen (10–14) business days unless the complainant files formal judicial proceedings.
