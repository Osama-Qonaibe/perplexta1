# Advertising Standards & Prohibited Creative Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Enforcing Authority: Advertising Standards Directorate, **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).

---

## 1. Advertising Philosophy & User Integrity

Advertising across the **Perplexta** platform and **ViralBook** community is engineered to connect verified enterprises with technical professionals and discerning consumers. We enforce strict creative standards to eliminate spam, predatory advertising, and fraudulent marketing practices.

---

## 2. Categorically Prohibited Advertising Categories

Sponsored campaigns, boosted posts, and promotional banner placements promoting any of the following categories are strictly barred:
1. **Deceptive Financial Schemes:** Get-rich-quick guarantees, unregistered cryptocurrency derivatives, binary options, and pyramid or multi-level marketing structures.
2. **Unregulated Pharmaceuticals & Health Claims:** Prescription-only medication dispensed without prescription, untested dietary supplements, and unverified miracle cures.
3. **Adult & Sexually Explicit Content:** Adult entertainment, pornography, escort services, or suggestive creatives.
4. **Unlicensed Gambling & Betting:** Unregulated online casinos, unauthorized sports betting operations, or unapproved prize draws.
5. **Malicious Payloads & Spyware:** Creatives prompting automated binary downloads, surveillance applications, or rootkit distribution.

---

## 3. Landing Page Integrity & Experience

Destination URLs submitted by advertisers undergo continuous automated sandbox evaluation prior to campaign launch:
- **Relevance & Consistency:** The destination landing page must directly reflect the claims, pricing, and offers promised within the creative.
- **No Deceptive Traps:** Destination pages must not contain recurring unclosable modal popups, deceptive audio playback, or forced redirection scripts.
- **Cryptographic Security:** Landing pages must serve valid SSL/TLS certificates over HTTPS and maintain zero active malware signatures.

---

## 4. Enforcement & Budget Forfeiture

ViralLinkUp Limited reserves the right to reject, suspend, or permanently terminate any advertisement violating these standards. Campaigns flagged for malicious fraud are subject to immediate budget forfeiture and permanent domain blacklisting.
