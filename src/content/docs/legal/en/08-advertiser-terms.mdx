# Master Advertiser Agreement & Campaign Terms

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

---

## 1. Scope & Advertiser Eligibility

This Agreement governs all commercial advertising placements, boosted posts, business page promotions, and sponsored campaigns across the **Perplexta** platform and **ViralBook** network. By initializing an advertising campaign or depositing campaign funds, you warrant that you are legally authorized to bind your commercial entity.

---

## 2. Campaign Delivery Metrics & Cost Accounting

1. **Pricing Models:** Campaign expenditures are calculated on an actual Cost-Per-Click (CPC), Cost-Per-Mille impressions (CPM), or flat daily boost pricing model.
2. **Real-Time Ledger Debiting:** Campaign expenditures are drawn in real-time from your prepaid ledger wallet. Ad distribution ceases automatically upon depletion of dedicated funds, guaranteeing zero unauthorized overdraft or deficit billing.
3. **Authoritative Metric Standard:** Platform server telemetry serves as the exclusive, legally binding authority for recording authentic impressions, valid clicks, and conversion events.

---

## 3. Commercial Verification (KYB Requirements)

- Advertisers deploying campaigns exceeding threshold budgets must complete Know-Your-Business (KYB) verification, including submission of corporate certificates of incorporation and tax identifiers.
- The "Verified Business" trust badge is conferred solely upon corporate entities completing documented identity authentication.

---

## 4. Campaign Cancellation & Dispute Resolution

Advertisers may pause or terminate active campaigns at any time via the ad management console. Unconsumed funds are immediately credited back to the advertiser's platform wallet balance. Cash refunds of unspent ad balances are administered strictly pursuant to our platform Billing & Refund Policy.
