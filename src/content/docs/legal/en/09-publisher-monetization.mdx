# Publisher Monetization & Invalid Traffic Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Regulatory Authority: Publisher Governance Division, **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).

---

## 1. Publisher Program Overview

The **Perplexta** and **ViralBook** Publisher Monetization Program empowers verified creators, technical authors, and enterprise media pages to earn revenue share from legitimate user interactions, certified impressions, and contextual ad placements.

---

## 2. Invalid Traffic Detection & Anti-Fraud Safeguards

The platform employs real-time anomaly detection heuristics to safeguard advertiser capital and creator ecosystem health:
1. **Artificial Engagement Ban:** Publishers are strictly prohibited from generating impressions, clicking their own advertisements, or orchestrating coordinated third-party click rings.
2. **Bot Filtering:** Traffic emanating from headless browsers, automated proxies, bot networks, or data-center IP pools is filtered in real-time and purged from payout metrics.
3. **Post-Delivery Audit Deductions:** Monthly revenues undergo secondary algorithmic auditing. Inauthentic impressions identified post-facto are deducted prior to disbursement.

---

## 3. Eligibility Criteria & Payment Settlement

- **Publisher Qualification:** Publishers must maintain active, unflagged accounts adhering fully to intellectual property standards and community guidelines.
- **Settlement Cycles:** Authorized earnings are settled on a Net-30 monthly cycle upon reaching verified platform minimum payout thresholds.
- **Compliant Disbursement Channels:** Disbursed payments are remitted strictly to verified corporate bank accounts or approved financial institutions matching the verified identity of the publisher.

---

## 4. Immediate Termination & Fund Forfeiture

Evidence of deliberate click-fraud, traffic manipulation, or syndicated impression generation triggers immediate permanent account termination, forfeiture of accrued illicit earnings, and compensatory restitution to affected advertisers.
