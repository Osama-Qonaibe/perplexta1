# Billing, Wallet Top-Up & Refund Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Billing & Financial Operator: **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).  
Authorized Payment Processors: Stripe Payments UK / Stripe Inc.

---

## 1. Prepaid Ledger Model & Instant Deductions

**Perplexta** implements an immutable, prepaid pay-as-you-go financial architecture tied directly to our dedicated Ledger Vault:
- **Operational Credits:** Wallets are funded with digital credit balances pegged directly to sovereign fiat remittances (£ GBP / $ USD).
- **Sub-Second Micro-Debits:** Workload requests (AI inference tokens, GPU execution seconds, ad impressions) are verified and deducted in real time, with transaction metadata logged immutably into the append-only ledger.
- **Strict Deficit Prevention (HTTP 402):** Invocations exceeding available ledger balance are rejected at the edge with HTTP status code `402 Payment Required`, preventing unauthorized debt accrual.

---

## 2. Definitive Refund Standards

Due to the irreversible, immediate provisioning of multi-tenant cloud compute and graphics hardware:
1. **Consumed Resources:** Credits consumed for executed workloads (token transfers, analytical parsing, GPU runtime, fulfilled ad impressions) are final, non-refundable, and non-creditable under any circumstances.
2. **Unconsumed Balances:** Users may request a refund of unconsumed, unallocated credit balances within fourteen (14) calendar days of the original deposit, subject to standard gateway processing fees and administrative deductions.
3. **Verified Infrastructure Outages:** Where engineering telemetry proves a service credit was debited without corresponding workload completion due to internal cluster fault, compensation credits are automatically remitted back to the user's platform wallet balance.

---

## 3. Anti-Chargeback Protocol

- Customers agree to contact Perplexta billing support to reconcile disputed charges prior to initiating financial institution reversals.
- Fraudulent or bad-faith chargebacks trigger immediate account termination, acceleration of owed fees, and reporting to cross-merchant risk mitigation databases.
