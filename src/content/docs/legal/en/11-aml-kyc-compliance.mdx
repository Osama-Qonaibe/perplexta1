# Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & KYC/KYB Compliance Framework

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Regulatory Authority: Financial Compliance Unit, **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).

---

## 1. Statutory Mandate & Regulatory Scope

**ViralLinkUp Limited** operates the **Perplexta** and **ViralBook** platforms under rigorous adherence to United Kingdom financial crime legislation, including:
- The Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017 (MLR 2017).
- The Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (POCA).
- The Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018.
- Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendations.

---

## 2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD) & Corporate Verification (KYB)

1. **Individual Verification (KYC):** Users exceeding cumulative wallet transaction thresholds are required to undergo automated identity verification matching government-issued photographic identity documents.
2. **Corporate Entity Verification (KYB):** Commercial publishers and high-volume advertisers must submit official Registry of Companies certificates, articles of association, and documentation establishing Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) controlling 25% or more equity.

---

## 3. Sanctions Screening & Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

- **Real-Time Screening:** All deposit sources and payout recipients are screened automatically against the UK Consolidated Sanctions List published by the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI), US OFAC lists, and EU/UN registers.
- **Sanctioned Jurisdictions:** Platform access and financial settlements are categorically blocked for IP blocks, bank accounts, or entities originating within comprehensively sanctioned territories.

---

## 4. Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)

The Compliance Unit reserves the statutory right to freeze suspicious balances and file formal Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) with the UK National Crime Agency (NCA) without informing the subject, in strict compliance with the statutory tipping-off prohibitions of English law.
