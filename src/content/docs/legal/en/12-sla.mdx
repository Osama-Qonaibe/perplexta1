# Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Uptime Commitment

Effective Date: September 21, 2026  
Infrastructure Operator: Cloud Operations Division, **ViralLinkUp Limited** (Company No: **16804604**).

---

## 1. Target Availability Commitment

**ViralLinkUp Limited** commits to commercially reasonable technical efforts to maintain a Monthly Uptime Percentage of not less than **99.9%** across all Perplexta core API endpoints, intelligent routing gateways, and core authentication services.

### 1.1 Calculation Formula
Monthly Uptime Percentage is computed on an annualized calendar month basis as follows:

$$\text{Monthly Uptime \%} = \frac{\text{Total Minutes in Month} - \text{Unscheduled Downtime Minutes}}{\text{Total Minutes in Month}} \times 100\%$$

---

## 2. SLA Exclusions

Downtime does not encompass service disruptions resulting from:
1. **Scheduled Maintenance:** System maintenance announced at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance and executed during designated low-traffic maintenance windows.
2. **Upstream Foundation Outages:** Global catastrophic failures impacting upstream foundational model infrastructure outside the Company's operational perimeter.
3. **Force Majeure & Mass Cyber Attacks:** Regional Internet routing collapse, international cable severance, or unprecedented volumetric DDoS attacks exceeding transit scrub capacities.

---

## 3. Service Credit Schedule

In the event of confirmed breach of our Monthly Uptime Percentage commitment, eligible enterprise customers may claim service credits calculated against the affected month's consumption:

| Verified Monthly Uptime | Service Credit Percentage Applied to Platform Balance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **99.0% to < 99.9%** | **10% of monthly billable consumption** |
| **95.0% to < 99.0%** | **25% of monthly billable consumption** |
| **< 95.0%** | **50% of monthly billable consumption** |

---

## 4. Credit Request & Claim Procedures

To be considered for a service credit, written claims containing request log IDs, timestamp ranges, and outbound error headers must be submitted to `sla@perplexta.com` within thirty (30) calendar days following the conclusion of the affected month.
