## 3. Modification Notice & Objection Mechanism

- Customers are notified via email or internal administrative security dispatch at least thirty (30) calendar days prior to the engagement of any new sub-processor.
- Enterprise customers maintain the contractual right to submit written objections on reasonable data protection grounds. Where mutual resolution cannot be reached, the customer may terminate affected service components without penalty.
